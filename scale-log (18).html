A weigh scale load log: enter a preprinted scale ticket number, truck, driver, activity, material, material type, variety, an optional crop cert number, optional grain moisture and test weight, and gross and tare weights on a ticket form to compute net weight and bushels. The origin field adapts to the selected activity: a field for harvest, a bin for transfer, sell, or seed own use, a supplier for purchase, or a free-text name for third party. The destination field is a bin for most activities, a field for seed own use, and is optional only for third party. View a bin capacity status table with warnings at 75 percent full, a bin inventory summary showing net weight in and out of each bin broken down by activity, a separate production tab summarizing harvest loads by field, and a running ledger of recorded loads with totals, editing, and reprinting.
